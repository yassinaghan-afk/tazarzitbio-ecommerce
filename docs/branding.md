## Brand promise

We deliver **100% natural** Moroccan staples — amlou, argan oil, honey, and curated nuts — sourced from the heart of **Souss**, with transparency, quality, and respect for tradition.

## Voice & tone

- **Warm** — like a trusted family brand from Agadir/Souss
- **Confident** — quality without arrogance
- **Clear** — short sentences; avoid medical or miracle claims
- **Proudly Moroccan** — local origin, local language first

### Do

- Emphasize origin (سوس), purity (طبيعي 100%), and craft
- Use concrete details: ingredients, weight, how to enjoy
- Build trust: harvest, preparation, packaging, COD simplicity

### Don't

- Overpromise health cures
- Sound like a discount marketplace
- Use generic “organic” fluff without specifics
- Mix too much French on primary Arabic surfaces (French OK for SEO/meta)

## Visual direction (summary)

Full tokens in `design-system.md`.

- **Feel:** Premium, earthy, clean — not rustic clutter
- **Palette:** Deep green (nature/trust), warm gold/amber (argan/honey), off-white backgrounds, charcoal text
- **Imagery:** Macro product shots, Souss landscapes, hands/craft sparingly
- **Typography:** Strong Arabic headline font + highly readable body; Latin for weights/SKU only if needed

## Trust signals (use everywhere)

- 100% طبيعي — no artificial additives (where true per SKU)
- من قلب سوس — geographic authenticity
- تغليف محكم — freshness
- الدفع عند الاستلام — COD, no online payment friction
- واتساب / هاتف للتأكيد — human reassurance (when live)

## Slogan usage

- **Primary lockup:** Tazarzit Bio + slogan under or beside logo
- **Hero:** Slogan may stand alone on home hero
- **Do not** translate slogan literally for Arabic headlines; keep **100% طبيعي من قلب سوس** as canonical
