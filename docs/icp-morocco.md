# ICP — Morocco (Ideal Customer Profile)

## Primary market

**Kingdom of Morocco** — nationwide delivery ambition with concentration in urban and peri-urban areas where COD logistics are reliable.

## Core segments

### 1. Urban household buyer (primary)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 28–50 |
| **Location** | Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Agadir, Fes |
| **Income** | Middle to upper-middle |
| **Behavior** | Orders via mobile Instagram/Facebook/Google; compares quality over lowest price |
| **Motivation** | Authentic amlou/argan/honey for family; trusts “from Souss” narrative |
| **Pain** | Fake or diluted products online; unclear delivery; fear of prepayment scams |
| **Message** | طبيعي 100% · من قلب سوس · الدفع عند الاستلام |

### 2. Gift & occasion buyer (AOV driver)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Triggers** | Eid, Ramadan, weddings, host gifts, corporate hampers |
| **Motivation** | Premium packaging, recognizable brand, easy COD for sender |
| **SKU focus** | Gift boxes, family packs, pistachio amlou tier |
| **Message** | هدية فاخرة من سوس — تغليف أنيق · توصيل لجميع المدن |

### 3. Diaspora & visitors (secondary)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Context** | Moroccans abroad ordering to family in Morocco, or tourists post-visit |
| **Motivation** | Ship trusted brand to parents; souvenir quality |
| **Constraint** | Delivery address must be Morocco for v1 COD |
| **Message** | أرسل لعائلتك في المغرب — منتجات أصلية من سوس |

### 4. Health-conscious foodie (tertiary)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Motivation** | Clean ingredients, no additives |
| **SKU focus** | Honey, argan oil, classic amlou |
| **Caution** | No medical claims; stick to natural/pure messaging |

## Psychographics

- Values **authenticity** and **local pride** (Souss, Berber/Amazigh heritage respectfully referenced)
- Willing to pay **premium** for provenance and packaging
- **Mobile-first** — expects WhatsApp-friendly communication
- **COD preference** — cash on delivery is default expectation in Morocco e-commerce

## Objections & responses

| Objection | Response (copy / UX) |
|-----------|----------------------|
| “Is it really natural?” | Ingredients on PDP, origin story, batch/season notes when available |
| “Delivery how long?” | City-based estimates on checkout |
| “I don’t trust online pay” | COD-only prominently |
| “Price vs souk” | Premium quality, sealed packaging, direct from brand |
| “Will they call me?” | Set expectation: confirmation call/WhatsApp before dispatch |

## Channels (acquisition)

1. **Instagram / Facebook** — visual product, UGC, reels from Souss  
2. **Google Search** — Arabic product intent (“أملو طبيعي”, “زيت أركان سوس”)  
3. **Word of mouth** — insert thank-you card in box (future ops)  
4. **Influencers** — micro food/lifestyle Morocco, not generic intl  

## Logistics reality (Morocco)

- Major cities: 2–5 business days typical (set in policy when carrier chosen)
- Remote areas: longer + possible surcharge — document in checkout
- Phone number validation critical (Moroccan formats)
- Arabic-first support hours in footer

## Personas (quick reference)

**Fatima, 38, Casablanca** — buys amlou + honey monthly for kids; discovers on Instagram; needs COD and clear delivery window.

**Youssef, 32, Rabat** — orders gift box for Eid to parents in Agadir; highest AOV; cares about packaging photo for approval.

**Nadia, 45, Marrakech** — quality-focused; reads ingredients; may call before first order.

## Not target (v1)

- B2B wholesale (future)
- Export without local fulfillment partner
- Price-shoppers only — brand is premium, not race-to-bottom

Align campaigns and homepage modules to **segment 1 + 2** first.
